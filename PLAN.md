**Debt-free on loans and personal debt: 15 November 2026 — 80 days from today.**

Rules that keep you out of a second loan:
- **Never borrow to repay a debt.** That is precisely how the ₱10,000 loan happened. One month of
  late fees is cheaper than one new loan.
- **Verify the lender is SEC-registered.** Unregistered lending apps have no legal right to the
  pressure tactics they use. Harassment, contact-list shaming and obscene messages are violations
  — the SEC and the National Privacy Commission both take complaints.
- **Get the total payoff figure in writing**, not just the installment. Some loans add fees at the end.
- **Screenshot every proof of payment.** Reference numbers, saved somewhere findable in six months.

each payday — if you wait until the end of the kinsenas the money is already gone. Keep ₱1,000–2,000
liquid in GCash or your wallet; a fund you can't reach in an hour isn't an emergency fund. Only
consider MP2 or time deposits *after* you pass ₱55,500.

## What could break this plan

Ranked by likelihood, with the response pre-decided so you don't have to think in the moment:

1. **Landlord refuses to split the deposit.** → You are ₱3,500 short on move-in day. Ask for a
   Sep 1 move-in, or cut essentials to ₱500 and pet to ₱800. Do not take a loan.
2. **A medical or vet emergency in October.** → Your lowest balance is ~₱1,300. This is why the
   ₱250/kinsenas medical line exists and must never be zeroed.
3. **The lender demands payment before Sep 15.** → Rent and food come first. A late fee is
   recoverable; losing your housing is not.
4. **Income arrives late.** → The ₱1,250 emergency-fund line is the shock absorber. Skip it for
   one kinsenas, never skip rent.
5. **Rent rises at lease renewal.** → At ₱7,000 you're already at 28% of income, the top edge of
   comfortable. Below 25% should be a condition of any renewal you sign.

## Using the app offline

One file, the whole app. No server, no account, no network calls.

**`index.html` — the entire app in a single 67 KB file.**
Markup, styles, logic and even the icon are inlined; the file has zero external
references. Email it to yourself, save it to your phone's storage, or keep it in
Drive — then just open it in any browser. Nothing to install, no hosting needed.
To put it online (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop), upload that one file and you're live.

The app used to ship as two builds (this single file plus a six-file PWA folder with
a service worker). The multi-file version broke when `index.html` was deleted, so the
orphaned sources (`app.js`, `styles.css`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon.svg`,
`build-standalone.js`) were removed. The newest build — with the paid-item **undo**
buttons and the live per-keystroke editing registry — is now the one and only app.

**Your data** lives in that browser's local storage on that device only — nothing syncs anywhere.
Use **Export backup** before clearing browser data, and **Import backup** to move between devices.
Note that localStorage on a `file://` page is per-browser and can be cleared with browser data, so
export a backup occasionally.
